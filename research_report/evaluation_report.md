# ABIDE-I ASD vs typical control: evaluation report (AAL-116 functional connectivity)

## Part B. Real experimental results (computed from saved out-of-fold predictions)

**Headline.** The 84-85% accuracy target was **not achieved** by any genuinely evaluated model. The best measured accuracy is **66.00%**
(pooled out-of-fold predictions, M3, seed 2026); the best seed-averaged accuracy is **65.33%** (M3). The best ROC-AUC is
**0.708**. All results are development cross-validation estimates, **not** independent-test performance.

**Cohort.** 600 ABIDE-I development subjects, 19 sites; 268 ASD (44.7%) and 332 typical controls; AAL-116 ROI time series (PCP, CPAC, band-pass 0.01-0.1 Hz, global signal
regression), 6,670 Pearson edges. Labels, sites, ROI order and fold assignment verified against the saved manifest (see `methodology_and_limitations.md`).
